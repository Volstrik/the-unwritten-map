# 🌙 THE UNWRITTEN MAP
### *A Mystery-Romance Game for the Talking Stage*
**Two Players · One Mystery · Unwritten Destiny**

---

## 🔮 Overview

**The Unwritten Map** is a real-time multiplayer web game crafted specifically for two people in a talking stage. It combines nature, mystery, curiosity, subtle flirtation, and romantic tension into an interactive journey.

As you and your partner explore the map, you unlock cards, take turns answering honestly, ask follow-up questions, dare each other, predict each other’s habits and future, and reveal sealed moonlight confessions.

---

## 🧭 The 5 Chambers of The Journey

1. **🌿 The Whispering Grove (Mystery & Chemistry Deck)**
   - 30 curated cards exploring first impressions, emotional safety, subtle attractions, and quirky endearing habits.
   - 2 PASS tokens per player (for when a question is too intimate or you want to save it).
   - 1 Follow-Up question allowed after every answer.
   
2. **🔥 The Starlit Glade: The Romantic Dare (Round 2)**
   - Elevating the tension!
   - Cinematic challenges: describe your ideal first date down to the scents and music, give an unfiltered 100% genuine compliment, plan a spontaneous midnight surprise date, or voice an unspoken truth.

3. **🔮 The Celestial Observatory: Predict Our Future (Round 3)**
   - Each player makes intuitive predictions about the other.
   - The other player marks them in real-time:
     - 😳 *Dangerously Accurate*
     - ✨ *Surprisingly True*
     - 😂 *Playful Denial*
     - 🌙 *Tell Me More...*

4. **🌑 The Eclipse Sanctuary: The Almost Confession (Round 4)**
   - Both players write their answer in secret to:  
     > *"Something about you that I’ve been wanting to tell you is..."*
   - Neither envelope opens until **both** are sealed under the moonlight.
   - When both seals break, both confessions shine simultaneously with a celestial chime!

5. **✨ The Unwritten Horizon (Destiny Chamber & Sparks Reading)**
   - Dynamic Chemistry Archetype (e.g., *The Celestial Alchemists*, *The Twilight Adventurers*, *The Moonlit Slow-Burns*).
   - Real-world next step date proposal generator (one-click copy to ask them out!).
   - Golden Rule: *"Whoever develops feelings first doesn’t automatically lose 😂"*.

## 📱 How to Play Across Different Devices

### 1. Same Wi-Fi / Local Network (Immediate & Zero Setup)
1. On your computer or phone running the server:
   - Open **`http://localhost:3000`** (or your Wi-Fi address `http://192.168.100.39:3000`).
2. Click **Create Room** and enter your nickname and persona.
3. You will receive:
   - A celestial room code (e.g. `MOON-420`).
   - A **Shareable Link** to copy and send via WhatsApp/iMessage/Telegram.
   - A **QR Code** that your partner can scan right from their phone camera!
4. As soon as both players connect, click **Unfurl the Map & Begin Journey**!

### 2. Free Cloud Deployment (Play Anywhere in the World)
You can deploy this repository to any free hosting provider in 60 seconds:
- **Render.com / Railway.app**: Connect this repo, start command `node server.js`, port `3000`.
- **Glitch.com**: Import repository and get a free instant `https://xxx.glitch.me` URL with live sockets.
- **Vercel / Netlify / GitHub Pages**: Static deployment automatically falls back to **WebRTC P2P (PeerJS)**, meaning two browsers connect directly across the internet with zero server config!

- **🌿 Canvas Fireflies**: Floating glowing particles with natural sine-wave breathing and touch ripples.

- **💬 Live Whispers Drawer**: Slide-out private chat for side comments, teasing, and playful banter during the game.
- **🕊️ PASS Tokens**: 2 physical tokens per player with sound effects.
